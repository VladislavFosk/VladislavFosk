## 👋 About Me

I work as a **maintenance technician**: hardware, equipment, diagnostics and repair are my daily routine. Programming is my newest passion. I'm learning step by step, and my goal is to build something of my own, even if it's small, made with my own hands.

I don't give up when things break. Debugging a stubborn bug or a project that refuses to compile is part of the craft, and every fix teaches me something. Programming is also my way to unwind and recharge.

When something goes wrong I can get frustrated and go quiet for a while, but I always come back to it. Stubbornness is my superpower. 💪
